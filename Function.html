Function Duel

Pick a function for each ball. Every 3 seconds each ball stops, shakes, and fires its whole graph across the field, left and right. A curve that touches the opponent takes off hit points.

Field: x and y from −10 to 10. A shot from (x₀, y₀) is the curve y = y₀ + f(x − x₀), drawn across the whole field in both directions.
