Tetrachord

Four keys, eight original songs. Each lane is a pitch band, low to high, so your fingers play the melody. Longer notes and accents arrive as chords of two or three keys at once. The bar under each song shows how its notes spread across the lanes.

Keys
Fall speed 100%
Timing 0 ms

Each song starts with its own key map. On a phone or tablet, tap the four lanes. If hits feel early or late with Bluetooth audio, move Timing until they line up. Fall speed only changes how fast notes drop, not the music. Esc pauses.

Paused

Tap or press a key as each note reaches the line. Press [ or ] to change fall speed.
