Tetrachord

Four keys, three sections: AI-generated songs, MIDI transcriptions, and your own MIDI files in Custom. In the AI songs each lane is a pitch band, low to high, so your fingers play the melody. Longer notes and accents arrive as chords of two or three keys at once; long notes in some songs are hold notes, so keep the key down until the tail reaches the line. The bar under each song shows how its notes spread across the lanes.

Each song starts with its own key map. On a phone or tablet, tap the four lanes. If hits feel early or late with Bluetooth audio, move Timing until they line up. Fall speed only changes how fast notes drop, not the music. Esc pauses.
