PIXEL FRONTIER

A compact, original survival sandbox · desktop controls Chop trees with the axe, dig with the pickaxe, build a workbench and furnace, then forge iron gear and summon bosses.

Inventory

Armor slot · Left-click: pick up / place · Right-click: take half / place one · Shift-click: quick move

Crafting

A/D move · Space jump · Left click use item (hold to keep swinging) · Right click place block · 1–0 / wheel hotbar · E inventory & crafting · H quick heal · F save · Esc pause
